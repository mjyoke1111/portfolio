# Product: mjyoke1111 portfolio

## What it is
The public home of mjyoke1111, someone who builds with AI in public. One page at https://www.mjyoke1111.biz.id that routes visitors to every project and shows, with real numbers, what has shipped lately.

## Projects (product truth)
- Model Reviewer (mr.mjyoke1111.biz.id): the same prompt to many AI models and coding harnesses; every raw output published side by side, failures included. 30 models, 3,122 runs, 565 of them not OK and still published.
- JevLab (jev.lab.mjyoke1111.biz.id): measured experiments with the Jev decision model; when does a typed model answer beat plain code. Each demo shows the real choice, probabilities and confidence.
- Labs hub (lab.mjyoke1111.biz.id): index of all lab projects.
- AI Game Studio (games.mjyoke1111.biz.id): 151+ browser games, each made by an AI model, model and harness credited, all playable.
- YouTube: AI Coding Arena (explainers, coding timelapses, shows) and Dil Mon Music (original AI-assisted songs and instrumentals).

## Audience
Developers, AI-curious builders and model watchers arriving from X, GitHub and YouTube, often on a phone. They want proof, not pitch: what did the models actually make, where did they fail.

## Voice
First person, plain, specific, honest. Numbers over adjectives. No hype words, no "welcome to my portfolio".
The portfolio shows finished, public work only (owner, 2026-10-23): no failure counts, staged or unfinished items, run ids
or internal labels. The raw failures live on Model Reviewer itself.

## Constraints
- Custom domains only in output; never a pages.dev URL.
- Static page, build-time data, Lighthouse 90+ mobile, < 120 KB gz excluding media.
- Never the whole word T-a-b in output. No personal real names, no emails.
- Mode: Experience (a showcase; the work leads).
